Decentralizing Trust in Ophthalmology

Ophthalmic advice and eye care myths are everywhere. AI nodes verify evidence URLs in real-time using the Equivalence Principle to curb visual health misinformation.

Why Irisyn Matters

By matching decentralization with non-deterministic LLM consensus, Irisyn solves the trust gap in online medical databases.

Authoritative Validation

Claims are cross-checked against peer-reviewed articles (PubMed, NIH/NEI, WHO, AAO) fetched live via non-deterministic contract nodes.

Financial Incentives

Proposers stake 1 GEN. If the verification consensus confirms their status, they double their stake. Incorrect proposals are slashed and burned.

Optimistic Democracy

Consensus is governed by GenLayer's unique protocol. Multiple independent validator nodes run queries, reaching democratic agreement.
